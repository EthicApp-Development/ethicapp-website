# New EthicApp research published in *The International Journal of Management Education*
*July 30, 2026*

We are pleased to announce the publication of **“Bridging intuition and reason: Exploring ethical decision-making through instructional design in case-based learning”** in *The International Journal of Management Education*.

The article, authored by Claudio Alvarez, Gustavo Zurita, and Antonio Farías, examines how a theory-informed instructional sequence delivered through **EthicApp** can support ethical decision-making in higher education.

## From intuition to reflection

Ethical judgments are not shaped by reasoning alone: intuition, emotion, duties, rationalization, and dialogue with others can all influence how people respond to a dilemma. Drawing on Schwartz’s Integrated Ethical Decision-Making model and Moral Foundations Theory, the study translates these dimensions into a sequence of case-based learning activities.

The research analysed repeated measures from 1,080 students at two Chilean universities as they engaged with an academic-misconduct dilemma in EthicApp. The findings show that different instructional prompts were associated with systematic shifts in students’ judgments: emotionally framed prompts increased willingness to help, while duty-oriented prompts reduced support for misconduct. Peer consultation also helped moderate more polarized positions.

## Implications for ethics education

The study shows how a process model of ethical decision-making can be turned into a concrete pedagogical design. Rather than presenting ethical norms in isolation, it offers guidance for structuring learning experiences in which students revisit a dilemma, justify their decisions, consider different perspectives, and reflect with peers.

This contribution reinforces EthicApp’s role as a digital environment for orchestrating structured, collaborative, and reflective ethics education.
