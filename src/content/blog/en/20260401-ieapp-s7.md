# IntelligentEthicApp: advancing toward a new generation of AI-supported ethics learning
*April 1, 2026*

The **IntelligentEthicApp** project aims to take the EthicApp platform to a new level in terms of pedagogical effectiveness by incorporating capabilities based on generative artificial intelligence. In particular, it seeks to strengthen key processes in ethics learning, such as formative feedback, scaffolding of moral reasoning, and support for ethical decision-making at both individual and collective levels.

This effort builds on an established line of research in the use of CSCL (Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning) technologies for ethics education, where EthicApp has proven to be an effective tool for structuring discussions around ethical dilemmas. However, several relevant limitations and challenges have also been identified, such as the tendency of anonymous discussion groups to reach premature consensus, as well as weaknesses in the argumentative skills of some students.

In this context, IntelligentEthicApp proposes a multi-agent AI architecture, where specialized agents can intervene at different moments of the learning process, promoting deeper reflection and greater argumentative diversity.

## Project development process

The project has followed a **Human-Centered Design** approach, involving researchers, educators, and developers in a structured co-design process.

This process has included multiple phases, such as:

- Identifying pedagogical needs based on real use of EthicApp.
- Generating and prioritizing ideas for the use of generative AI.
- Defining functionalities and requirements.
- Designing a multi-agent architecture.
- Developing prototypes.
- Conducting formative evaluation with users.

This process has extended over more than one year and involved more than 20 participants from different disciplines and educational contexts.

Among the main ideas that emerged from the co-design process are:

- Automated interventions to improve the quality of discussions.
- A “devil’s advocate” agent to promote productive disagreement.
- Promotion of relevant topics during discussions.
- Automated evaluation and feedback on individual arguments before sharing them with peers.

## Prototype evaluation (Session 7)

A recent milestone of the project was **Session 7**, dedicated to the evaluation of prototypes derived from the co-design process.

In this session, two main tools were evaluated:

1. **Argumentation Tutor**  
   A system that allows students to construct and refine their arguments through cycles of automated feedback. The tutor is based on Toulmin’s model (claim, evidence, and warrant), guiding students in structuring their justifications.

2. **“Devil’s Advocate” Agent**  
   An agent that participates in group discussions by introducing counterarguments, challenging ideas, and promoting reflective disagreement. Its goal is to prevent premature consensus and foster deeper analysis of ethical dilemmas.

During the session, participants engaged with both systems using the **Sebastián Case**, an academic ethics dilemma widely used in previous studies. The activity included guided interaction, group discussion, and final evaluation of the tools.

Preliminary results from this evaluation indicate that:

- The **argumentation tutor** shows high acceptance in terms of usability and learning support.
- The **chat mediation agent** shows significant potential, although its effectiveness strongly depends on the timing and quality of its interventions.
- There is broad agreement on the importance of maintaining pedagogical control in the hands of the instructor.

## Outlook

The project will continue with new iterations of design, student-based evaluation, and refinement of the prototypes, with the goal of integrating these developments into the EthicApp platform.

IntelligentEthicApp is expected to contribute to the advancement of AI-based educational systems that do not replace teachers or students, but rather enhance their agency, fostering deeper, more reflective, and well-grounded ethical learning.
