# RITSI Award at JENUI 2025 for EthicApp Research
*July 2025*

The paper *“Development of ethical reasoning in Computer Science using active methodologies supported by the EthicApp platform”* was presented at the **XXXI Conference on University Teaching in Computer Science (JENUI 2025)**, held in Valladolid, Spain.

The presentation was delivered by **Alejandra Martínez Monés**, Full Professor at the University of Valladolid and researcher at the GSIC/EMIC research group (Intelligent and Cooperative Systems / Education, Media, Computer Science and Culture).

The work, developed through a collaboration between the University of Valladolid, the University of Chile, and Universidad de los Andes, was awarded the **RITSI Best Paper Award**, granted by the Spanish Association of Computer Science Students (RITSI).

## About the paper

The paper presents a teaching innovation project aimed at fostering ethical reasoning skills in computer science students through active learning methodologies supported by **EthicApp**.

The approach combines case-based learning and computer-supported collaborative learning to address key challenges in ethics education, such as engaging students in meaningful discussions and monitoring the development of their ethical reasoning over time.

As part of the project, several learning designs were created and implemented across different educational contexts (undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral levels). These designs integrate individual reflection, written argumentation, and structured group deliberation.

Initial results show positive perceptions from both students and instructors, highlighting increased student engagement and the usefulness of the platform in supporting ethical discussions, particularly in large classroom settings.

## Relevance

This recognition at JENUI 2025 highlights the potential of **EthicApp** as a tool for ethics education in computer science, especially in a context where ethical training is becoming increasingly important due to the societal impact of artificial intelligence and digital technologies.

The work also contributes to ongoing efforts to integrate ethics into computer science curricula, aligning with international recommendations and emerging educational needs in higher education.
