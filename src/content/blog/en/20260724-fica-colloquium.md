# Presentation at the Faculty of Engineering and Applied Sciences: ethical micro-insertions in engineering
*July 24, 2026*

On Friday, July 24, Claudio Alvarez presented **“Ethical micro-insertions in engineering: scaffolding deliberation through Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning and Artificial Intelligence”** in the **Research Colloquium at the Faculty of Engineering and Applied Sciences at Universidad de los Andes, Chile**.

The presentation discusses EthicApp and a research agenda for ethics education in engineering. It considers ethical deliberation as a design problem, models of ethical decision-making, teachers’ orchestration load, technical debt, and how artificial intelligence can support augmented deliberation.
