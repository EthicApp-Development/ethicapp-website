# Article on AI-Supported Ethical Deliberation Published in J.UCS
*November 28th, 2025*

We are pleased to announce the publication of a new research article by **Claudio Álvarez**, **Gustavo Zurita**, and **Andrés Carvallo** — researcher at the National Center for Artificial Intelligence (CENIA, Chile) — in the *Journal of Universal Computer Science (J.UCS)*.

The article explores the integration of generative artificial intelligence into collaborative ethics education, with a particular focus on how AI-based agents can support ethical deliberation, argumentation, and reflection processes in higher education contexts.

The work builds upon the ongoing development of **EthicApp** and the broader **IntelligentEthicApp** initiative, which investigates human-centered approaches for incorporating generative AI into pedagogically grounded learning environments.

The publication discusses both the opportunities and the challenges associated with AI-mediated ethical learning, including issues related to argument quality, orchestration of collaborative discussions, and the design of responsible forms of human–AI interaction in educational settings.

The article is available through the Journal of Universal Computer Science at:

[https://lib.jucs.org/article/152864/](https://lib.jucs.org/article/152864/)
